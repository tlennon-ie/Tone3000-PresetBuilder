### Prince — *Purple Rain* (1984)
**Genres**: Pop Rock, R&B, Power Ballad
**Equipboard Reference**: [https://equipboard.com/pros/prince](https://equipboard.com/pros/prince)

- **Guitars**: Hohner MadCat Telecaster (flamed maple body, vintage single coils)
- **Pedals / Front-end**: Boss DS-1 Distortion, Boss BF-2 Flanger, Boss OC-2 Octave
- **Amplifier**: Mesa Boogie Mark V / Mark II Series (Lead Channel)
- **Cabinet / Speakers**: 1x12 Mesa Combo (Celestion Black Shadow C90 / EVM12L)
- **Outboard Compression / EQ**: Teletronix LA-2A Tube Optical Compressor
- **Reverb / Space**: EMT 250 / Lexicon 224 Plate Reverb (32% wet)
- **Notes**: Iconic high-sustain 80s lead tone. DS-1 drives the front of the Mesa Mark lead channel; compressed for endless sustain into lush studio plate.
